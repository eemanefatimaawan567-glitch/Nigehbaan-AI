Technology

How Nigehbaan Works

📡

Multi-Channel Sensing

Each zone combines four environmental channels: rainfall intensity, river gauge level, soil saturation, and sub-surface acoustic anomaly — capturing both flood and landslide precursors simultaneously.

🧮

Dual Risk Scoring

Separate flood and landslide sub-scores are computed with weighted sensor inputs and slope data. An overall score blends both hazards — so a zone doesn't need to fail just one threshold to be flagged.

🚑

Rescue Prioritization

Zones are ranked by rescue priority — a function of overall risk and population density. High-risk rural areas and dense urban flood plains are ranked independently, so no affected community is overlooked.

🔔

Alert Dispatch

Operators can issue multi-channel alerts — SMS, Rescue 1122, NDMA coordination — directly from the command centre. Every alert is logged with timestamp, level, and zone for accountability and audit.
